MoEngage Session & Configuration

Manage your MoEngage cookies, cluster endpoints, Gemini API keys, and automation schedules

How to get your MoEngage Session Cookies in 30 seconds
  1. Open dashboard-01.moengage.com (or your cluster) in Chrome/Firefox and log in.
  2. Press F12 (or Cmd+Option+I) to open Developer Tools.
  3. Go to the Network tab and click any request (e.g., segments or app/info).
  4. In the Headers pane under Request Headers, right click the Cookie: value and click Copy value.
  5. Paste the copied cookie text into the box below and click Test Connection.

Cookies are kept strictly in your local SQLite database on your machine and never transmitted anywhere other than your MoEngage cluster.
